# Opportunity Provider & Discovery Policy

## Terms of Service Boundaries
- CivicLens only crawls registered authoritative public domains, RSS feeds, Sitemaps, and official APIs.
- CivicLens NEVER bypasses CAPTCHA, authentication paywalls, anti-bot mechanisms, or anti-scraping controls.
- Sites blocking automated access are marked `BLOCKED` and directed to official API / feed integration paths.

## Product Scope Standard
- CivicLens never claims to index "the entire internet" or "all government opportunities."
- The product promise: **"CivicLens continuously discovers and verifies opportunities from registered government, institutional, and private sources, then routes citizens to the authoritative source to apply."**
