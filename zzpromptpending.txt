# CIVICLENS OPPORTUNITY INTELLIGENCE EXPANSION

## Build a Unified Government + Private Opportunity Discovery Engine

You are working on the existing CivicLens repository.

This is an expansion of CivicLens.

Do NOT create a separate application.

Do NOT replace the existing scheme/eligibility architecture.

Extend CivicLens into a unified platform that automatically discovers, structures, verifies, deduplicates, updates, and presents opportunities from authoritative government and permitted private sources.

The platform must cover:

```text
Government Jobs
Private Jobs
Internships
Apprenticeships
Scholarships
Fellowships
Government Schemes
Benefits
Grants
Training Programs
Skill Programs
Job Fairs
Competitions
Admissions where appropriate
Other legitimate public opportunities
```

The system must provide users with one searchable, personalized opportunity feed.

---

# 1. CORE PRODUCT REQUIREMENT

CivicLens must become:

```text
A unified opportunity discovery engine
```

The user should NOT need to visit dozens of websites just to discover opportunities.

CivicLens should discover opportunities from registered sources and present them in one normalized interface.

However:

CivicLens MUST NOT falsely claim that it has indexed "the entire internet."

The system should only claim:

```text
Indexed Sources
Verified Sources
Last Crawl Time
Last Verification Time
```
